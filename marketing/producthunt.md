## Description longue (maker's note / first comment)

```
Hi Product Hunt 👋

I built HealthWatch Global because epidemic surveillance data is scattered across 4+ sources in formats that make operational decisions slow.

**What it does:**
- Aggregates WHO Disease Outbreak News, ECDC CDTR, PAHO & Africa CDC daily
- Classifies each outbreak into IHR response tiers: Immediate (notifiable under IHR Article 6), Rapid (24-72h assessment), Monitor (ongoing surveillance)
- Email alerts on new outbreaks matching your watchlist
- Available in English, French, Spanish, Arabic, and Indonesian

**Who it's for:**
National IHR focal points, epidemiologists at NGOs and ministries, and field health teams who need to know what's happening before it's in the news cycle.

**Why it matters:**
WHO publishes Disease Outbreak News, but there's no tool that translates "new Ebola cluster in Équateur Province" into a concrete decision tree for a focal point in a neighboring country. That's the gap we fill.

**Current data coverage:**
- WHO DON (daily, 4x/day sync)
- ECDC Communicable Disease Threat Reports (weekly)
- PAHO regional alerts (weekly)
- Africa CDC (2x/week)

Free tier available. Pro plan for advanced alerts and full disease history.

Happy to answer questions about the IHR framework integration or the data pipeline.
```

## Réponses aux questions fréquentes anticipées

**"How is this different from HealthMap or ProMED?"**
> HealthWatch focuses on official WHO/ECDC sources with IHR compliance layer, not RSS aggregation. The IHR tier classification is unique — it tells you specifically what action is required under international law, not just what's happening.

**"Is the data real-time?"**
> Data syncs 4x daily from WHO DON (6h, 12h, 18h, 0h UTC). WHO publishes DONs irregularly — we capture new ones within 6 hours of publication.

**"Why 5 languages?"**
> IHR national focal points are in 196 countries. The majority work in French, Spanish, Arabic, or Indonesian — not English. A monitoring tool that only works in English misses most of the people who need it.

**"Is there an API?"**
> Not yet — it's on the roadmap for institutional/ministry integrations. Contact us if you need it now.

**"Free vs Pro?"**
> Free: dashboard access, 3 watchlist items, daily digest. Pro (€49/month): unlimited watchlist, instant alerts, full disease history, IHR tier filter.
